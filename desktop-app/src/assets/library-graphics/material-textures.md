# Material Library textures

Carbon Weave, Obsidian Slate and Hammered Titanium were generated for NEO-LIB
using the built-in image-generation tool. Full-bleed 1024×1536 PNG originals are
retained here as `*-original.png`. Runtime WebP copies use quality 88/effort 6
without resizing; 160px-wide picker thumbnails use quality 84/effort 6.

Unlike the transparent illustrations, these are opaque physical material surfaces.
The existing background opacity controls their blend with the theme. Full-height
placement fills the entire Library backdrop; none paints above text or screenshots.
They are static images, not animated textures or 3D scenes.

## Final prompts (verbatim)

Shared prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production background texture for a tall desktop game-library panel, portrait 2:3. Full-bleed macro material surface continuously covering every pixel top to bottom, flat orthographic face-on camera, even material scale across the whole image. Restrained luxurious real texture, medium-low contrast, neutral charcoal and silver so the chosen app theme can show through via its opacity slider. Readability behind white small game labels is important. No scene, no objects, no UI, no text, no logos, no border, no central vignette, no huge hotspots or fake depth-of-field blur. Crisp detailed texture, not a 3D product mockup.

### carbon-weave

Subject: tightly woven black carbon fiber, fine diagonal twill with alternating graphite strands, subtle cool satin highlights, precise tactile weave. Uniform lighting; not a giant checkerboard. Elegant high-tech real material.

### obsidian-slate

Subject: dark charcoal volcanic slate, layered fine stone grain, irregular delicate fracture veins and small mineral flecks; subtle natural silver-gray ridges. Broad full-surface variation, no large slabs or separate rocks, no gold cracks. Rich tactile stone.

### hammered-titanium

Subject: brushed gunmetal titanium with fine small hammered dimples, soft overlapping microfacets, subtle directional satin brushing and a few faint warm silver reflections. Quiet continuous industrial metal surface; no rivets, bolts, grid seams or objects.
