# FiFi rig generation prompts

Built-in image-generation tool; reference: `../poses/fifi-idle-v1.png`.

## Body

Transparent cutout PNG game sprite. A single perfectly round glossy purple and neon magenta plasma orb, cyan glow along bottom right, soft electric nebula texture inside. Straight-on circular ball alone, no eyes no appendages no face. Match the body material of the reference character, but completely bare sphere. Center at x50% y50%, diameter 80% of canvas. All surrounding pixels must be fully transparent alpha zero. Isolated object only.

## Fin

Use case: stylized-concept. Single transparent animation rig asset: ONLY ONE of FiFi's smooth curved petal fins from the character reference. No creature or orb. A luminous translucent cyan and violet curved leaf-shaped glass fin with fine turquoise wireframe grid, broad near upper left and tapered tip at lower right, isolated centered full fin with padding. Premium polished 3D rendering matching reference. Real alpha-channel transparency outside fin. No background, no checkerboard, no body, no eyes, no hands, no text, no extra fins. The fin will be independently rotated and mirrored around a game mascot.

## Tendril

Transparent cutout PNG animation rig part for referenced FiFi character. ONLY ONE small hanging binary-code tassel: glossy purple-cyan rounded cuff at top with five delicate curved cyan strands of glowing 0 and 1 digital glyphs hanging down. No fingers, no hand, no arm. Short flowing data ribbon/tassel matching the character's lower tendrils. Vertical composition centered with wide empty padding. Premium 3D neon hologram rendering. Actual transparent alpha pixels outside the cuff and strands, no background, no checkerboard, no orb, eyes or fins. This part will sway beneath a digital floating mascot.
