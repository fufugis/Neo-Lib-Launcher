# NEO-LIB v1.8.7 — Main additions since v1.8.5

Theme maintenance: Moonlit Arcana has brighter blue-violet surfaces, clearer text and luminous cyan/lilac accents. Desktop artwork is slightly less darkened, with its soft presentation and original full-resolution image preserved.

Naming maintenance: Visual tweaks is now Library tweaks in navigation, its panel and tutorial guidance. Controls and saved preferences are unchanged.

Sidebar controls: Visuals now includes Sidebar tweaks for spacing, icon size, color saturation, button order and an icons-only layout with immediate hover labels. Settings are saved locally; reset restores the standard grouped sidebar.

Sidebar editing: the sidebar stays expanded and highlighted beside its tweaks panel, without dimming or blur over the live preview. Closing the panel restores your chosen sidebar mode.

Sidebar sizing: a separate Button size slider scales the complete buttons from 75–150%, including width, height, text and padding. Icon size remains separately adjustable, and the preview highlight/editor clearance follow the resized sidebar.

- **Home layout editor:** Move, resize and park Home widgets; extend page height and scale content. Lounge adds grouped Visuals, compact FX, custom themes, pinned previews and cover/info heroes.
- **Game-preview layout editor:** Rearrange and resize section boxes; content reflows at normal text size instead of shrinking the whole preview.
- **Playtime calendar:** Month/year heatmaps for Home and Lounge, with game filters and recorded daily playtime.
- **News and mail inbox:** Team announcements appear once as a popup and remain in your inbox until deleted.
- **Automatic idle power saver:** After 15 quiet minutes, pauses expensive visuals while keeping mascot announcements available.
- **New welcome and tutorial:** Guided setup or exploration, a refreshed tutorial, optional 16+ usage reporting and privacy notice. Permanent sidebar navigation and a simpler tabbed Wizard.
- **Community widget coding guide:** Import widget includes starter files and clear format, API, permission and capability guidance.
- **Update popup and download:** New GitHub releases show a download prompt even when the mascot is disabled.
- **Retro emulator setup:** Add emulators and ROM folders row by row, track time per ROM and optionally exclude ROMs from statistics.
- **Library background graphics:** Three 3D graphics, full-height textures, Ember/Camo artwork and object-shaped Industrial, Future and Stoneage cursors. Custom Lounge stills keep originals and scene effects.

These additions are prepared for v1.8.7, not the published v1.8.6 installer. Publication and installed Windows/UI acceptance remain separate. Small adjustments and technical details are recorded below and in PROGRESS.md / WORK_QUEUE.md.

Storage maintenance: Home and individual Library size scans share saved local measurements. Sizes reappear after restart, update across views, and remain intact when a scan fails or skips a game.

Maintenance record: retained Lounge previews, cached Library shelves and rows, and compositor-driven grids preserve visual settings while avoiding repeated work. Actual CPU savings still require a controlled live comparison.

Navigation maintenance: Add games has a prominent, separate spot directly below Lounge, with Wizard underneath and the same setup flow. Rest/Wake moved into the Exit section, directly above Quit; its behaviour is unchanged.

Toolbar maintenance: the All launchers picker now lives inside Sort. The same launcher choices and active filter are preserved; Sort is highlighted when filtering by a launcher. Both menu surfaces are fully opaque, with no transparency fade; embedded choices stay in flow without overlapping the sort options.

Cursor maintenance: Petal is a pointed flower petal, Industrial is a steel drill, and Future is a sci-fi craft—not decorated standard arrows. Retro has crisp pixel edges, preserving the saved Pixel selection. Click tips stay aligned; previews, text, disabled and resize cursors remain functional.

Lounge maintenance: Viewing distance is now a highlighted five-step slider with two intermediate sizes, clear labels and explanations. Existing Near/Medium/Far selections retain their sizes.
